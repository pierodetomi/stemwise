### Common setup of the measures (proposal)

- **A single analysis STFT** for bands, centroid, activity, stereo and masking: periodic Hann window,
  `n_fft = 8192` at 44.1/48 kHz (≈170 ms, resolution 5.9 Hz: the 20–60 Hz band has 7 bins), doubled at 88.2/96 kHz
  to keep the same duration; hop `n_fft / 4`. Frames from the start of the file, last frame completed with zeros.
- **Level of a frame** = Hann-weighted mean square `Σ w²x² / Σ w²`, averaged over channels, in dBFS
  (`10·log10`). By Parseval it equals the sum of the bin powers, so bands and activity are consistent.
- **Activity (Q-14)**: active frame if level > −50 dBFS. Band activity (Q-16): band level of the frame
  > −50 dBFS.
- **Bands (ANA-3)**: bins with `lo ≤ f < hi` (the last band includes 20 kHz, and is limited to `fs/2`); energy =
  mean power over active frames; dB relative to the sum of the 8 bands, so `Σ 10^(b/10) = 1`.
- **Energy on the bus** (ANA-8, ANA-9): mono `x²`, stereo `L² + R²`, i.e. the power the stem carries on the stereo
  bus under the law of D-008.
- **Memory**: the STFT is computed in blocks and immediately reduced to powers per frame and band, centroid and
  the L/R cross-spectrum; the full spectrogram is never kept, so memory is dominated by the decoded stems (already
  loaded in M0).
- **Precision**: measures in full float64 precision; rounding to one decimal and the ±120 dB limits (Q-18) apply
  only when writing `analysis.json`.

### Steps

Each step: tests first, implementation, `make check` green, commit `M1: <what> (<ID>)`. Each step updates
`docs/analysis.md` with the formula, parameters and version of the measures it introduces.

1. **Dependencies and spectral core** (ANA-3, ANA-4 in part). `uv add librosa pyloudnorm` (stack of PRD §7, needs
   the author's approval); check of the `macosx_arm64` wheels and of the `pyloudnorm` API (Q-15). New module
   `analysis/spectral.py`: STFT grid, frame level, activity, energies per frame and band, ANA-3 bands,
   `active_pct`, centroid. Helper `pink_noise()` in `tests/helpers/signals.py`.
2. **Loudness** (ANA-1 LUFS, ANA-2). New module `analysis/loudness.py`: integrated with `pyloudnorm`,
   K-weighting with the BS.1770 formulas, 3 s short-term with 100 ms step, LRA (Q-15).
3. **Levels and stem hygiene** (ANA-1, ANA-6). In `analysis/stem.py`: RMS on active frames, crest, DC
   offset, clipping, noise floor.
4. **Onsets, stereo and full stem measurement** (ANA-4, ANA-5). Onsets with `librosa`, correlation and side/mid
   from the cross-spectrum; `measure_stem()` that collects ANA-1 → ANA-6.
5. **Mix** (ANA-7, ANA-8, ANA-10). `analysis/mix.py`: measures on the raw sum, shares per band, `low_corr`.
6. **Masking** (ANA-9). `analysis/masking.py`: scores per pair and band, top 10.
7. **References and target** (ANA-11, ANA-12). `analysis/reference.py`: normalization to −23 LUFS, measures,
   mean profile.
8. **`analysis.json` contract** (ANA-13). `analysis/model.py`: Pydantic models, conversion with rounding and
   limits, JSON writing; `schemas/analysis.schema.json` with anti-drift test; token budget.
9. **Orchestration, CLI and acceptance**. `analysis/analyze.py` (`analyze_project()`), `runs.analysis_run()`,
   the `analyze` command, M1 acceptance tests, "Usage" section of the README.

### Minor choices proposed (unless objected)

- **Extra modules** compared with PRD §8: `analysis/spectral.py` (STFT and activity shared by stems, mix and
  masking), `analysis/loudness.py`, `analysis/model.py` (contract) and `analysis/analyze.py`
  (orchestration). Like `runs.py` in M0, they avoid duplicating code across the PRD modules.
- **Fields of `analysis.json`**: those of PRD §6.2, plus those that ANA-1 and ANA-7 ask for but the example does not
  show: `rms_db` per stem and mix, `peak_db` and `crest_db` for the mix. Key `version: 1` at the top, as in
  `mix_plan.json`. Band labels in the masking as in the example: `"20-60"`, …, `"500-2k"`, …, `"8k-20k"`.
- **References**: `analysis.json` has a `references` list with the file (path relative to the project) and the ANA-7
  measures of each, besides `target`. The LUFS of a reference is −23 by construction.
- **Crest factor** = sampled peak − RMS on active frames (not true peak).
- **Centroid** weighted by power (not by amplitude as `librosa.feature.spectral_centroid`), on the mean power of
  active frames, 20 Hz–20 kHz: a single formula, easy to reproduce.
- **Onsets**: `librosa.onset.onset_detect` with default parameters, on the signal reduced to mono (channel mean);
  parameters and `librosa` version written in `docs/analysis.md`.
- **Entirely inactive stem** (silence or below −50 dBFS): RMS, crest, bands, centroid, stereo and masking `null`;
  onsets 0; `active_pct` 0.
- **File format**: indented objects, arrays of numbers on a single line (more readable and shorter than standard
  indented JSON); keys in the contract order, stems in the order of `project.yaml`.
- `mix --baseline-only` stays as it is: it does not write `analysis.json`.

### Done

- **`stemwise analyze <song>`** writes `runs/<date-time>/analysis.json` in a new run folder (D-019). The folder
  is created only if the analysis succeeds and is removed if writing fails. `stems/` and `references/` are never
  touched.
- **Spectral core** (`analysis/spectral.py`). A single STFT per signal (Hann, `n_fft` 8192 at 44.1/48 kHz, hop
  ¼), reduced in blocks to:
  - powers per frame and band;
  - mean spectrum of the active frames;
  - stereo sums.
  On this basis: active frames above −50 dBFS in 20 Hz–20 kHz (D-014, D-022), 8 bands (ANA-3), `active_pct` and
  centroid (ANA-4).
- **Loudness** (`analysis/loudness.py`): BS.1770 integrated LUFS and EBU Tech 3342 LRA with `pyloudnorm` 0.2.0
  (ANA-1, ANA-2, D-015).
- **Stem** (`analysis/stem.py`):
  - RMS and crest on active frames;
  - peaks and true peak from M0;
  - onsets per active second with `librosa` (D-020, D-023);
  - correlation and side/mid (ANA-5);
  - DC in %, clipped samples, noise floor (ANA-6);
  - `measure_stem()` that collects everything.
- **Mix** (`analysis/mix.py`): same measures on the raw sum with the trim, i.e. `baseline.wav` (ANA-7, D-017);
  shares per band on the stem sum (ANA-8); `low_corr` 20–120 Hz (ANA-10).
- **Masking** (`analysis/masking.py`): min/max score frame by frame per pair and band; the 10 strongest collisions,
  in deterministic order (ANA-9, D-016).
- **References** (`analysis/reference.py`): normalization to −23 LUFS, ANA-7 measures, mean target profile
  (ANA-11, ANA-12). In the JSON the references appear only as `ref1`…`ref3` (D-024).
- **Contract** (`analysis/model.py`):
  - Pydantic models with ±120 dB limits, no `inf`/`NaN` (D-018);
  - one decimal, two for correlations and scores (D-021);
  - JSON with the lists of numbers on one line;
  - `schemas/analysis.schema.json` checked against drift.
- **Documents**: `docs/analysis.md` with formula, parameters and version of every measure; README ("Usage").
- **Tests**: 192, all green (+89 compared with M0), with synthetic signals and fixed seeds. Duration: a full analysis of
  4 stems of 30 s takes about 4.5 s; `measure_stem` on a 4-minute stereo stem about 4.7 s (for 12 stems of 4 minutes
  an estimate of about 1 minute).

- **Minor choices from the plan, confirmed**:
  - modules `spectral.py`, `loudness.py`, `model.py`, `analyze.py`;
  - `version: 1`;
  - `rms_db` per stem and mix, `peak_db` and `crest_db` for the mix;
  - crest = sampled peak − RMS;
  - power-weighted centroid.

### Doubts and risks

- **The fixed thresholds are choices, not reference measurements.** They are −50 dBFS for activity, 2 dB for onsets and
  1 s for the noise floor. On synthetic signals they work; the manual validation will tell whether `active_pct` and
  `onsets_per_s` are plausible on real voice, pads and drums.
- **K-weighting of `pyloudnorm`.** It uses the RBJ approximation of the BS.1770 filters: a 997 Hz sine at −20 dBFS reads
  −23.05 instead of −23.01 LUFS, within ±0.1 LU. A commercial meter may differ by a few hundredths.
- **Frame resolution.** Activity has the granularity of a frame of about 170 ms. The edges between sound and silence
  shift the RMS by about 0.04 dB and `active_pct` by 1–2 points.
- **Python 3.11.** The lock installs `librosa` 0.11.0: onsets may differ. The reference values are with
  Python 3.12, pinned in `.python-version`.
- **`low_corr` ahead of M4** (note 7 of the review). There is no level threshold in 20–120 Hz: a mix with almost no bass
  gives a "residual" correlation, and EVA-4 (> 0) could flag a violation with no meaning.
  To be decided at the start of M4.
- **Bleed on close microphones** (emerged in manual validation, decided by the author: noted and not corrected in M1).
  On snare, toms and hi-hat the rest of the drums enters above −50 dBFS, so `act%` (84–89%) and
  `ons/s` (2.7–3.2) describe the bleed more than the instrument. The bands show it too: the hi-hat has its energy peak
  at 120–250 Hz, that is the snare. The model will have to infer it from the role; an activity threshold relative to the
  stem level remains an option for later.
- **Collisions between microphones of the same drum kit** (manual validation): snare–overhead and overhead/hi-hat–bus
  in the highs are "physical" collisions between microphones of the same instrument, not musical conflicts. To be
  told to the model in the M3 prompt.
- **Minimal collisions.** With few stems the top 10 may include collisions with a positive score but below 0.005,
  written as `0.0`: it is noise, and the model can ignore it. They can be filtered with a minimum threshold, if in M3
  they prove misleading.
- **Process.** The commit of step 4 started with the format check failing, because `| tail` hid the
  exit code. It was corrected immediately with an amend, before any other commit; from then on `pipefail` is used.
