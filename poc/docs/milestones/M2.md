### Common setup of the render (proposal)

- **Pure function** `render_mix(stems, plan, options) -> MixResult` on in-memory arrays; the CLI reads and writes the files.
- **Precision**: stems in float32 (ING-6); biquads in float64 (`scipy.signal.sosfilt`); compressor and reverb from
  `pedalboard` work in float32 (the library does it). Bus sum in float64, result in float32, as in M0.
  No internal clipping: up to the trim, samples may exceed ±1.
- **Chain per stem (REN-2)**, fixed order: pre-gain → high-pass → low-pass → EQ (bands in the plan's order)
  → compressor → makeup → gain → pan → send to the reverb. A `null` block (or `eq: []`) is skipped entirely: the
  signal passes unchanged, sample by sample.
- **Pre-gain (REN-1)**: `g = −23 − LUFS_stem` with the integrated loudness from M1 (`pyloudnorm`, BS.1770-4). A mono
  stem measured at −23 LUFS stays at −23 LUFS on the stereo bus too, because the √½ center per channel preserves the
  power. With `pre_normalize: false` the pre-gain is 0 dB.
- **Filters and EQ (Q-25 a)**: RBJ biquads from the Audio EQ Cookbook, form with Q. High-pass and low-pass second-order
  Butterworth (Q = 1/√2, 12 dB/octave); `peak`, `low_shelf`, `high_shelf` with `A = 10^(dB/40)`.
  Same biquads for the high-pass on the reverb return.
- **Compressor**: `pedalboard.Compressor(threshold_db, ratio, attack_ms, release_ms)`, then makeup as pure gain. No knee:
  the library's own compressor, documented as such (PRD §7.1, warning about the port).
- **Pan (REN-3)**: mono stem at constant power, `L = x·cos θ`, `R = x·sin θ`, `θ = (p+1)·π/4` (at the center √½, the
  law of D-008); stereo stem with the balance of Q-26 (a). Written in numpy.
- **Reverb (REN-4)**: post-fader and post-pan send (order of REN-2), `send = stem_stereo · 10^(send_db/20)`;
  sum of the sends → `pedalboard.Reverb(room_size, damping, wet_level=1, dry_level=0, width=1)` → RBJ high-pass at
  `return_hpf_hz` → `return_gain_db` → bus. With `reverb: null`, or with all sends `null`, no reverb.
  Duration equal to the stems (Q-27 a).
- **Bus (REN-5)**: sum of the processed stems + reverb return, then the same trim as M0 (D-007). No other processing.
- **Neutral plan**: gain 0, pan 0, all blocks `null`, `reverb: null`, `pre_normalize: false` ⇒ `mix.wav`
  identical to `baseline.wav`, bit for bit. It is the test that ties M2 to D-008.
- **Version**: `render v1` in `docs/analysis.md` (new section "Render"), with formulas, coefficients, mapping of the
  plan parameters onto the plugins, and the version of `pedalboard`.

### Expected doubts

- **Stereo compressor.** From the JUCE code the `BallisticsFilter` detector keeps state per channel: it is likely that
  the `pedalboard` compressor reduces the two channels independently, slightly shifting the image of stereo stems
  (overheads, doubled guitars). I will verify it at step 4 with a test; if so, I propose accepting it for the PoC and
  noting it (a linked compressor written in numpy would leave `pedalboard`, so I would ask first).
- **Reverb.** JUCE's Freeverb sums the stereo input to mono before the combs and scales wet and dry internally:
  the position of the send (pre or post pan) hardly changes anything, and the return level must be set with
  `return_gain_db`. I note it in `docs/analysis.md` with the measured values.
- **Determinism.** `pedalboard` says nothing about it; no random generator in the plugins used and
  `process(..., reset=True)` zeroes the state at every call. The hash test verifies it between two runs.
- **Out of scope to keep out.** Compressor knee, delay, saturation, sidechain: not foreseen (PRD §3).

### Doubts and risks

- **Unlinked compressor** (D-034). On stereo stems very unbalanced between channels (overheads, doubled guitars on a
  single stem) the image may shift during compression. To be listened to in validation.
- **Reverb with high level.** The return comes out from +0.8 to +6.8 dB above the send depending on `room_size`
  (measured, in `docs/analysis.md`): the plan's sends must be thought out with this in mind. I will tell the model
  in the M3 prompt.
- **Pre-gain without ceiling** (D-033): a nearly empty stem (for example an effect that sounds for a few seconds) can
  rise by many dB and bring the noise with it. There is a warning above +24 dB.
- **Reproducibility tied to `pedalboard`.** The hash is stable with the version pinned in `uv.lock` (0.9.26); an
  update of the library can change the samples of compressor and reverb, so `render v1`
  (finding 6 of the review; warning about the port of PRD §7.1).
- **Cost of `analyze`.** With `pre_normalize: true` the STFT of the stems is computed twice (raw measures and
  normalized interactions): about twice the analysis time of the stems.
- **Exported stems and antiphase.** If two stems cancel each other in the mix, the individual exported files may exceed
  0 dBFS and be clipped: the mix stays correct, the sum of the files does not. There is a warning.
- **Deviations from the plan**: the tolerance on the sum of the exported files is 1 LSB per file (not ½), because of the
  scale asymmetry of libsndfile already measured in M0. Some tests have names or files different from those of the plan:
  the trim tests with plan are in `tests/render/test_render.py` instead of `test_mixbus.py`;
  `test_reverb_keeps_duration` became `test_reverb_keeps_duration_and_is_deterministic`; the tests of invalid plans from
  CLI are two (`test_mix_with_invalid_plan_fails`, `test_mix_with_plan_above_nyquist_fails`).

       role, p = s["role"], dict(neutral)
       stems[s["id"]] = dict(neutral)
