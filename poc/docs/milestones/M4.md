### Common setup

- **Loop** (PRD §4): analysis and raw sum computed once, then for k = 0 … `max_iter` − 1: decision (with the history of
  the last 2 iterations and their evaluations, DEC-1; change limits of DEC-5 relative to the previous plan) → render →
  evaluation → stop criterion. Each iteration writes `iter_NN/` (`plan.json`, `mix.wav`, `evaluation.json`, decider
  log); at the root of the run: `baseline_evaluation.json`, `best.json`, `report.md`.
- **Modules** (PRD §8): `evaluate.py` (EVA-1 → EVA-4, pure functions on measurements and spectra), `loop.py` (EVA-5,
  EVA-6: stop criterion, best-plan selection, loop driver with decider, render and evaluation injected), `report.py`
  (REP-1: report text from data read from the run's files). `runs.py` remains the only point that writes files;
  `cli.py` remains thin.
- **Contracts**: `evaluation.json` and `best.json` are born as Pydantic models and go into `schemas/` (Q-41).
  Evaluation formulas in `docs/analysis.md`, new section "Evaluation" (`evaluation v1`).
- **Computational cost**: re-analysis of each iteration redoes the STFT of every processed stem (for residual
  collisions) and of the mix; spectra are computed during render, one stem at a time, without keeping the tracks in
  memory (Q-46). The raw sum reuses the spectra from the analysis.
- **Decider**: the `Decider` interface does not change. The system prompt gains a section on how to read the history
  (`spectral_distance_db`, sign of `band_delta_db`, constraints, residual collisions, `is_best`). The fake gains the
  feedback rule of Q-49 (a).

### Anticipated doubts

- **H5 and loop cost** (D-047). A full `mix` makes up to 3 calls, and from M4 each carries the history (up to 2 plans
  and 2 evaluations, estimated ~3,000 extra tokens on calls 2 and 3). With Sol at medium effort the estimate per `mix`
  is ~0.15–0.20 USD, before the 2 revisions of protocol §10.2. The report will show the actual cost; the decision on
  H5 (threshold, count or default model) is yours after manual validation.
- **Computation time.** On the real track the analysis takes ~3.5 minutes; each iteration adds render and STFT of 13
  processed stems plus the mix, estimated 1.5–2 minutes. A 3-iteration `mix` can reach ~10 minutes beyond the calls.
  I measure it in validation; optimizations (for example a single STFT per spectrum and measurements) only if needed
  and without changing the formulas.
- **Chasing the target** (PRD §11). Spectral distance drives the stop and the best choice, but does not tell whether
  the mix sounds better: listening does (H3, M7). The report must make it easy to compare by ear the raw sum, iteration
  0 and the best.
- **Collisions dominated by drums** (doubt from M3). Residual collisions too will almost all be between drum
  microphones; the report will show the top 5 as they are. A per-instrument-family filter would change ANA-9: outside
  this milestone, to propose separately if needed.
- **Flagged out of scope — keep out.** No stop criterion based on listening or on the model, no numerical optimization
  of parameters (the plan is decided only by the decider), no rollback in `revise` (M5), no chart in the report.

### Doubts and risks

- **H5 (cost) still to measure** on the real track with the full loop: it is step 3 of manual validation. With 3
  iterations the history adds plans and evaluations to calls 2 and 3.
- **Computation time not measured on the real track.** Each iteration redoes render and STFT of the processed stems and
  of the mix; the raw sum adds one STFT per mono stem (D-058). Estimate: several minutes beyond the ~3.5 of M3.
- **Distance ≠ quality** (PRD §11): distance drives the stop and the best choice, but a mix closer to the reference is
  not necessarily better. Listening decides (H3, M7).
- **Dynamics on synthetic projects**: with 1 s stems the dynamic range (LRA) is not measurable and `dyn_delta_lu` is
  `null`; on real material it is present.
- **Edge cases recorded without correction** (review findings):
  - a mix with a target but no active frames would be labeled `no_target` (finding 4, silent mix);
  - an error that is not a decision error at an iteration k ≥ 1 (for example a full disk) is not covered by D-055: with
    the fake the folder is removed, with the real model it stays without `report.md` and with `stop_reason: null`
    (finding 5);
  - without valid iterations the report shows no per-stem reasons, because D-056 limits them to the best plan
    (finding 7).
- **Flagged out of scope — collisions dominated by drums** (doubt from M3): remains so also for residual collisions.
- **Deviations from the plan**:
  - the brief is not in the report: it is not in any file of the run and D-056 wants the report from the run's files
    only;
  - no trim column, because it is not saved in the files (the mix true peak is);
  - `test_mix_with_plan_writes_iteration (extended)` became `test_cli.py::test_mix_with_plan_writes_a_report`
    (plus the updated file list in `test_mix_with_plan_writes_iteration`);
  - EVA-1 on the raw sum is also verified in `test_evaluate.py::test_baseline_evaluation_reuses_the_analysis`;
  - the real loop test (`test_llm_live.py::test_real_model_runs_the_loop`) is new, outside `make check`.
