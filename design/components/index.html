Guided tour

The spotlight and the bubble of the tour (P-020, screen 18), with the opening and closing cards.

Spotlight

Inspector
The hole is the target grown by --tour-pad (4), with the radius of a card (--r-3) and a 2 px ring in the phase accent; around it --tour-dim, lighter than a dialog’s scrim so the context stays readable. Inside the hole the app works (Play, a fader, a block); outside it clicks do nothing. A hole never leaves the window: on a full-width area it is pulled in so the ring shows.

The inspector

The chain of the selected track, in the order the sound goes through it. Open a block to change it, or switch it off to hear the difference.

3 of 15
Tracks on a timeline

Each imported file becomes a track, laid out under the sections of the song. Editing never touches your files, and every change can be undone.

Width --tour-w (340). Counter and a bar of fifteen marks (done in the accent, the current one light); title in the display face; one or two sentences; an optional “Try it” line when the lit area invites a gesture; Skip tour, Back, Next. The arrow (--tour-arrow) is cut from a square on the side facing the target and points at its anchor. Right: focus on Next, where it lands at every step.

Bubble · waiting

8 of 15
A proposal to review

Nothing changes until you decide. Read why each track changes, compare by ear with A/B, then apply all of it, only the tracks you pick, or none.

Preparing the proposal…a few seconds
A step that needs the proposal or the mixdown before it is ready: the text stays, a status row says what is being made (azure when the assistant makes it, the phase accent otherwise), Next is off until it is done; then the step shows the result by itself.
