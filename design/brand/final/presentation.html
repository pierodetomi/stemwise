Stemwise · mark · refined direction D

Two rooms

The two trajectories of proposal D are the Mix: many parts that approach and come together. Beyond a gap is the Master, a single solid shape: the finished song. First the lines, then the room; first the lime, then the lilac.

The mark is made of two separable parts, room-mix and room-master: in the top bar the room of the phase you are in lights up.

1 · From proposal to refinement

Same trajectories, another story

The curves are those of D: horizontal run, curve, straight diagonal, curve, horizontal run, with continuous tangents. What changes is what they do: they come close without merging, and the arrow gives way to the second room.

→
The flawHow it is solved
1It reads as the generic “merge” icon.The two lines no longer end in a common arrow: they come close and hand the song to a separate, solid shape. The mark is a bracket that gathers, followed by a room.
2The two lines arrive together, like two equal inputs.The two trajectories are now both the Mix (lime): many parts that come together. The Master (lilac) comes after, beyond a gap, and reads from left to right.
3The arrowhead is a cliché and at 32 px it becomes a blotch.The arrow is removed. In its place the Master room, a solid rounded rectangle, separated from the lines by a gap of 7.5/100.
4The mark occupies half the height.The mark spans 15.5 to 84.5 in height and 4.5 to 95 in width: two thirds of the square, instead of half.
5In monochrome the two rooms disappear.The Mix is made of lines, the Master is a solid shape: two rooms recognizable even in a single color. In the bar the room of the current phase lights up.
6The lockup lettering is not Bricolage Grotesque.The name is Bricolage Grotesque 600, tracking −0.01 em, converted to outlines from the same font file the app includes.
7The icon background is not a squircle.The background is the macOS continuous-corner squircle: an 824 px body centered on the 1024 square, with the shadow in the margin.

2 · Construction

100 grid, stroke 9

The two Mix lines

They start at 20 and 80 and arrive at 45.5 and 54.5: at the end they touch without overlapping (stroke 9, rounded caps and joins). Each curve has its control points at x = 31 and x = 45; the diagonal segment takes the central 40 % of the path between the two control points.

The Master room

A solid rectangle of 30 × 26 with radius 10, centered on the axis: taller than the two lines side by side (18) by almost one stroke, so that at a glance it weighs as much as the Mix. The radius is soft like that of the app's cards. The gap between the lines and the room is 7.5.

Footprint

From 4.5 to 95 in width, from 15.5 to 84.5 in height: the mark is centered in the square, wider than tall like the left-to-right reading it tells.

Optical versions

At 20, 32 and 16 px the mark is redrawn on the pixel grid: horizontal runs on whole rows, 2 px stroke (1.5 at 16), gap of at least 1.5 px, room with a 2 px radius.

7 · Clear space and minimum size

Half a room all around

The clear space is x, half the height of the Master room (13/100 of the mark's side), measured from the visible mark. In the lockup the same x applies, measured on the mark; between mark and name the distance is fixed (15 units with the name at 40).

Minimum sizes

Mark16 px. Under 24 px the optical drawings are used: mark-20.svg in the bar, favicon-32.svg and favicon-16.svg in the browser.
Lockup110 px wide (name about 12 px tall). Smaller: the mark only.
IconAll macOS sizes from 16 to 1024 from the same icon-1024.svg.

9 · Verification

The six criteria

RecognizabilityA lime bracket that hands over a lilac room: two simple shapes and a gap, which are remembered. At 16 px, in the favicon and in the icon, lines, gap and room remain visible.
RelevanceIt tells the two phases in order: many parts that come together (Mix), then a single solid thing (Master). It uses no audio symbols.
Without colorLines against a solid shape: the two rooms remain two even in monochrome, and the top bar tells them apart by position.
ConsistencyRounded stroke like the app's icons, soft radii like cards and clips, phase accents, Bricolage Grotesque in the name.
CalmTwo colors on aubergine, no pure white or black, no effects; the gradient lives only in the icon background.
DurabilityEssential geometry, with no references to fashions or technologies: it holds as long as the product has two phases.
