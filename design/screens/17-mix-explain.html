Platereverb return

What it is

A shared effect: a plate reverb with a 1.2 s tail, kept between 300 Hz and 12 kHz. Tracks do not pass through it: each one sends a copy, and only the reverb comes back, into the mix bus, at −12 dB.

Who sends here

4 tracks

is the wettest, the driest. The other 8 tracks send nothing here.

What it does for the mix

Tracks that share a reverb sound as if they were in the same room. The more a track sends, the further back it sits: here the backing vocals are behind the lead.

What happens to it, in order

REV
Plate reverb1.2 s · pre-delay 20 ms

The tail starts 20 ms after the sound, so words stay clear in front of it.

HP
High-pass300 Hz

No reverb on the low end: bass and kick stay tight.

LP
Low-pass12 kHz

A darker tail, with no hiss on the s sounds.
