Stemwise design

15 · Master chain

P-017. The chain is a rack beside the editor: a list of blocks, top to bottom. Input comes first, Limiter and Dither last; everything in between can be put in any order, and EQ, Compressor, Multiband, Saturation and Clipper can be there twice (twelve blocks at most). New: a clipper, parallel compression and a detector filter on the compressor, a multiband compressor with two to six bands, offsets per section, a proposal that shows what it measured, and a way back to the mix when the target asks too much. Replaces the module row of 9a–9c; the rest of screen 9 stands.

15a

The rack, clipper open

One row per block with its value, the gain reduction it applies and its switch; the number is the note of the assistant. A lock marks the three blocks that stay in place: the limiter must be last to guarantee the ceiling, and dither follows it. “Add module” is always in sight, where a new block lands. The editor of the selected block is beside the rack. The Loudness card gains the clipper meter and how long the limiter works over 3 dB.
