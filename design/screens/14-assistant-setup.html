14 · Assistant setup

Connecting the assistant without copying a key: the app opens OpenRouter in the browser and receives a key made for it. Then credit, and the manual path for who already has a key. Opened from the first launch (1b), from Settings › AI (11b) and from the assistant column when no key is set (13e).

What is OpenRouter?A service that gives one account access to many AI models. You pay OpenRouter for what the model uses, a few cents per proposal. Stemwise adds nothing on top and has no account system of its own.
Opens openrouter.ai in your browser. Sign in or create an account there, then approve Stemwise. A key made for this app comes back by itself and is kept in the macOS keychain.
Your audio never leaves this MacThe model receives measurements, roles, track names, your brief and your requests. Never audio, file names, paths or the song title.

Add credit on OpenRouterCredit is prepaid: you add an amount once and each request takes a little from it. Nothing is charged while you are not using the assistant.
One mix proposal≈ $0.1012 tracks, 2 passes
One revision≈ $0.04
$5 of credit≈ 40 mixeswith the default model
Stemwise cannot see your balance: OpenRouter does not share it with apps. If the credit runs out, the assistant says so at the next request and nothing in your mix changes.
